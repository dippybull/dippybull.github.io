  1. 목적 — Dippy Uploader는 채널 운영자 본인의 유튜브 채널(@dippybull)에 영상을 올리고 예약 공개를 설정하는 데만 쓰입니다.

Privacy Policy (English)

Dippy Uploader is an internal tool used only by the channel owner to upload and schedule videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (@dippybull). It uses YouTube Data API upload/manage scopes, authorized only by the owner. It does not collect, store, or share anyone else's personal data. OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Contact: via the @dippybull YouTube channel page.
